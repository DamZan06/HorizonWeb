ABOUT HORIZON

HORIZON was created as a personal test of endurance.

HORIZON is a complete crossing of Switzerland from east to west. The concept is deliberately simple: start at Piz Chavalatsch, keep moving west, finish at Chancy. The website exists to make the journey visible — through live GPS tracking, telemetry, photographs and field notes.

What HORIZON is

HORIZON is a long-distance human-powered crossing of Switzerland from the easternmost point to the westernmost point. The route covers approximately 500 km with more than 10,000 m of positive elevation gain.

Motivation

The project is a test of endurance, consistency and navigation. It is not a competition; it is a personal challenge to move steadily toward the horizon, one kilometre at a time.

Preparation

Preparation includes training, route study, gear selection and logistical planning. The platform and tools used to share the journey were developed to prioritize reliability and minimalism.

Equipment

Lightweight but safe equipment is essential. GPS, emergency gear, batteries and technical clothing are balanced to ensure autonomy and safety across variable alpine terrain and weather.

Nutrition

Sustaining long hours of movement requires careful energy and hydration management: water, electrolytes and high-energy food are consumed to maintain performance and recover between days.

Route

The route crosses lakes, valleys, forests and alpine passes. North is up: the route follows the actual GPX and is not altered for visual reasons.

Support

The journey includes logistical support when needed. The website focuses on transparency: live tracking, recorded data and media will document the crossing without embellishment.

KEEP MOVING WEST.
