ONE COUNTRY. ONE DIRECTION.

HORIZON is a two-person, long-distance crossing of Switzerland from east to west.

The journey begins at Piz Chavalatsch, at the eastern edge of the country, and ends at Chancy in the west. Between the two lies roughly 500 kilometres of roads, trails, valleys, mountain terrain, darkness, fatigue and uncertainty.

There is only one direction for us: west, together.

WHY HORIZON?

The horizon is always ahead.

We never reach it by staring at it. We reach it by moving toward it.

HORIZON is the idea of continuing forward as a team, even when the destination is still far away.

One kilometre becomes the next. One step becomes another. Until the horizon becomes the finish line.

KEEP MOVING WEST.

500 kilometres. More than 10,000 metres of climbing. More than 200 hours of movement.
