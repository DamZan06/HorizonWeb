ONE COUNTRY. ONE DIRECTION.

HORIZON is a two-person, long-distance crossing of Switzerland from east to west.

The journey begins at Piz Chavalatsch, at the eastern edge of the country, and ends at Chancy in the west. Between the two lies roughly 500 kilometres of roads, trails, valleys, mountain terrain, darkness, fatigue and uncertainty.

There is only one direction for us: west, together.

WHY HORIZON?

The horizon is always ahead.

We never reach it by staring at it. We reach it by moving toward it.

HORIZON is the idea of continuing forward as a team, even when the destination is still far away.

One kilometre becomes the next. One step becomes another. Until the horizon becomes the finish line.

KEEP MOVING WEST.

THE CHALLENGE

What is HORIZON?

HORIZON is an extreme long-distance journey across Switzerland, completed on foot from east to west. It begins near Piz Chavalatsch in the far east and reaches Chancy in the far west, crossing a country that changes every day in terrain, weather and rhythm.

The expedition is continuous, human-powered and shared by two people travelling together. It is not a short route or a fast push. It is sustained movement through distance, elevation, fatigue, recovery and uncertainty, with the route itself creating the challenge.

HORIZON exists to be followed in real time: the website is the public record of where we are, how much is complete and what the journey demands from day to day.

THE PEOPLE

The two people behind HORIZON

Damiano Zanoni

ENDURANCE · MOUNTAINS · ALWAYS FURTHER

For Damiano, HORIZON is the next step in a journey that has kept getting longer, harder and more ambitious. From mountain biking to extreme endurance challenges across Switzerland, every experience has pushed the limit a little further. HORIZON brings all of it together: more than 500 kilometres on foot, from one edge of Switzerland to the other, with no certainty except that stopping will eventually feel easier than continuing. And that is exactly where it gets interesting.

Nicolas Mooser “NicMos”

ADVENTURE · CHAOS · QUESTIONABLE IDEAS

Nicolas, better known as NicMos, is the kind of person who rarely needs a good reason to say yes to a questionable idea. HORIZON is exactly that kind of idea. More than 500 kilometres across Switzerland, on foot, with plenty of opportunities for things to go wrong along the way. He brings energy, unpredictability and the willingness to keep moving when common sense might suggest otherwise. Two people, one route, and probably more than a few bad decisions before Chancy.

THE PHILOSOPHY

The distance is only part of it.

HORIZON is not about proving that two people are exceptional. It is about deliberately entering a situation where the outcome is uncertain and discovering what is required to continue. The expedition is a test of attention, adaptation, endurance and trust in the process.
